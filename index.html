Our Story

Sweet Treats, Real Heart

🇺🇸 Veteran-Owned 👩 Women-Owned 🏳️‍🌈 LGBTQ+ Friendly

We're a local Jacksonville treasure — proudly veteran and women-owned — bringing the sweetest late-night experiences to Argyle Forest, Oakleaf, and West Jacksonville.

From our signature stuffed shakes to our freshly baked cookies, everything is crafted with care and served with a warm welcome that keeps families coming back again and again.

Late night ice cream in Argyle Forest just got better — now open Monday through Thursday until 9 & Friday and Saturday until 10!
