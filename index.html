Our Story

Sweet treats, real heart

🇺🇸 Veteran-Owned 👩 Women-Owned 🏳️‍🌈 LGBTQ+ Friendly ♿ Wheelchair Accessible

We're a charming, family-owned ice cream parlor in the heart of Argyle Forest — proudly veteran and women-owned. We're not just serving ice cream; we're crafting unforgettable moments in an old-time parlor atmosphere.

We serve Hershey's premium hand-dipped ice cream with 56 rotating flavors, over 20 toppings, and endless combinations. Whether you're stopping in for a quick scoop or settling in for a stuffed shake and fresh-baked cookie, every visit feels like a celebration.

We also carry taffy, fudge, and vintage candy — and for those 21+, our boozy shakes are a crowd favorite. The pups aren't left out either, with our special doggie cup!

🍦56 Hershey's flavors including gluten-free, vegan, dairy-free, lactose-free & no-sugar-added options
🧂20+ toppings to customize your treat exactly how you like it
🍬Taffy, fudge & vintage candy also available in the shop
📸Photo op wall inside — perfect for family visits & date nights
♿Wide aisles for easy wheelchair access throughout the shop
