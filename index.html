Our Story

Sweet treats, real heart

Veteran-Owned Women-Owned LGBTQ+ Friendly Wheelchair Accessible

We're a charming, family-owned ice cream parlor in the heart of Argyle Forest — proudly veteran and women-owned. We're not just serving ice cream; we're crafting unforgettable moments in an old-time parlor atmosphere.

We serve Hershey's premium hand-dipped ice cream with 50+ rotating flavors, over 20 toppings, and endless combinations. Whether you're stopping in for a quick scoop or settling in for a stuffed shake and homemade cobbler, every visit feels like a celebration.

We also carry taffy, fudge, and vintage candy — and for those 21+, our boozy milkshakes are a crowd favorite.

50+ Hershey's flavors including no-sugar-added, lactose-free, and vegan options
20+ toppings to customize your treat exactly how you like it
Taffy, fudge & vintage candy also available in the shop
Photo op wall inside — perfect for family visits & date nights
Wide aisles throughout for easy wheelchair access
